Case studies

Situations that come up at the trade finance desk. Read the facts, decide for yourself, then tap to see the answer and the rule behind it.

Case 1

Goods description on the invoice

Field 45A of the LC reads '100% cotton men's shirts, sizes M to XL'. The commercial invoice describes the goods as 'Cotton shirts'. The bill of lading says 'Shirts'.

Discrepant or not?

Show the answer
Discrepant

The invoice must correspond with the credit's description (Art. (c)). It doesn't have to be a word-for-word copy, and details can appear in different places on the invoice. But 'Cotton shirts' leaves out that they are 100% cotton, that they are men's, and the size range. Read as a whole, the invoice doesn't describe the goods the credit calls for. The bill of lading is fine: documents other than the invoice may describe the goods in general terms that don't conflict with the credit (Art. (e)).

Case 2

Presentation after 21 days

The LC expires on 30 April and states no presentation period. The bill of lading shows shipment on board on 2 March. Documents are presented on 28 March.

Discrepant or not?

Show the answer
Discrepant

With no stated period, original transport documents must be presented within 21 calendar days after shipment, which here ends on 23 March. The expiry date being later does not help. See Art. .

Case 3

Refusal on the sixth banking day

Documents are presented to the issuing bank on Tuesday, 10 November 2026. The bank, in India, finds a genuine discrepancy. It asks the applicant whether they will waive it, waits for a reply, and sends its refusal notice on Wednesday, 18 November.

Can the bank still refuse?

Show the answer
Cannot refuse

Counting starts the day after presentation, and only banking days count: Wed 11 (1), Thu 12 (2), Fri 13 (3). Sat 14 is a 2nd Saturday and Sun 15 a Sunday, so both are skipped. Mon 16 (4), Tue 17 (5).

The fifth banking day ended on Tuesday, 17 November, so a notice on the 18th is late. Asking the applicant for a waiver does not extend the time (Art. (b)). Because the notice was late, the bank is precluded from claiming the documents do not comply (Art. (f)), even though the discrepancy is real. In practice, it has to honour the presentation.

Lesson: start the waiver request early, and send the refusal notice within five banking days whatever the applicant says.

Case 4

Short shipment of bulk goods

The LC is for 500 metric tons of wheat, USD 100,000, partial shipments not allowed, with no 'about' wording. The seller ships 490 metric tons and invoices USD 98,000.

Discrepant or not?

Show the answer
Not discrepant

Wheat in bulk is not counted in packing units or individual items, so a 5% quantity tolerance applies, provided the amount drawn does not exceed the credit. 490 tons is within 5%. See Art. .

Case 5

Insurance dated after shipment

The bill of lading shows goods shipped on board on 10 May. The insurance certificate is dated 12 May and does not say cover is effective from the date of shipment.

Discrepant or not?

Show the answer
Discrepant

The insurance document must be dated no later than shipment, unless it shows that cover is effective from a date no later than shipment. See Art. .

Case 6

Different address for the applicant

The LC shows the applicant's address in Noida. The commercial invoice shows the applicant's address in Gurugram. Both are in India. The address does not appear in the consignee or notify party field of the transport document.

Discrepant or not?

Show the answer
Not discrepant

Addresses of the beneficiary and applicant need not match the credit, as long as they are in the same country. The exception is when the address appears as part of the consignee or notify party details on a transport document. See Art. .
