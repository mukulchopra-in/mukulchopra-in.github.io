Privacy

Privacy notice

What this site collects, why, and what it never does. In short: very little, and nothing is sold or shared.

Last updated 10 November 2026

The short version

  • No cookies, no advertising and no tracking across other sites.
  • The LC decoder and the calculators run entirely in your browser. Nothing you type or paste into them is sent anywhere.
  • If you use the contact form, your name, email and message are used only to reply to you.
  • Visits are counted anonymously, to see which pages are useful.

Contact form

When you send a message, the form passes your name, email address and message to Web3Forms, a form-delivery service, which forwards it to my email inbox. I use your details only to reply to you. I don't add you to any mailing list, and I don't sell or share your details. Web3Forms handles the message in transit under its own privacy policy.

If you'd like me to delete our email conversation, ask in a message and I will.

LC decoder and calculators

Everything you paste into the LC decoder, and every figure you enter into a calculator, is processed in your own browser. It is not uploaded, sent or stored, and it is gone when you close or refresh the page.

The decoder's "Get an AI prompt" button only builds text for you to copy. Nothing is sent to an AI service unless you paste it into one yourself. If you do, that service's own terms apply; remove client names first, and check your employer's policy.

Visitor statistics

The site uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits. It sets no cookies and does not build a profile of you. It records things like the page visited, the site that referred you, your country, browser and device type, and how fast the page loaded. On the LC decoder page it records only that the page was visited, never what you pasted.

Hosting and fonts

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, and its fonts load from Google Fonts. As with any website, these services receive your IP address when your browser requests a page or a font, and may keep it in their logs for security and operation, under their own privacy policies.

Cookies and storage

This site sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser.

Links to other sites

Links to LinkedIn, Instagram and other sites take you to services with their own privacy policies.

Questions

For any question about your data, or to ask me to delete our correspondence, use the contact form.
