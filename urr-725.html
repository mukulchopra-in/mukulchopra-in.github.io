URR 725 · Uniform Rules for Bank-to-Bank Reimbursements

Bank reimbursements

When a nominated bank pays under an LC, it often claims its money from a third bank, not the issuing bank. URR 725 governs that claim.

Based on ICC Publication No. 725 · Last reviewed October 2026

  1. Issuing bank → Reimbursing bankAuthorisationIssuing bank authorises the reimbursing bank to pay claims under the credit.
  2. Claiming bank → Reimbursing bankClaimAfter paying the beneficiary, the nominated bank sends its claim.
  3. Reimbursing bank → Claiming bankPaymentReimbursing bank pays and debits the issuing bank.

Key provisions

Applicability

Stated in the authorisation

URR 725 applies when the reimbursement authorisation says so. It does not override the credit's terms between issuing bank and beneficiary.

Independence

Separate from the credit

The reimbursement authorisation is separate from the credit. The reimbursing bank is not concerned with the credit's terms and does not examine documents.

No certificate of compliance

Claims stand alone

A reimbursement authorisation should not require the claiming bank to certify that it has complied with the credit's terms.

Processing time

Three banking days

The reimbursing bank has up to three banking days following the day it receives a claim to process it.

Charges

Normally for the issuing bank

Reimbursing bank charges are for the issuing bank's account. If they are to be borne by another party, the credit and authorisation must say so.

Reimbursement undertaking

An optional extra commitment

If the issuing bank asks and the reimbursing bank agrees, the reimbursing bank can give the claiming bank its own irrevocable undertaking to pay claims.

Expiry

Authorisations should not expire

A reimbursement authorisation should not carry an expiry date or a latest date for claims, except where the reimbursing bank has given a reimbursement undertaking.

Interest claims

Settled between the banks

A claim for loss of interest because of late reimbursement is a matter between the claiming bank and the issuing bank, unless the reimbursing bank has agreed otherwise.
