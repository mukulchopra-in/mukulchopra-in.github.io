LC date calculator

Work out the last day to present documents, the bank's examination deadline, and whether shipment was in time.

Enter bank holidays at the place of presentation. Results are only as accurate as the dates you enter.

If the LC sets no period, UCP 600 Art. 14(c) applies 21 calendar days after shipment for presentations that include original transport documents, and never later than expiry. Art. 29(a) moves a last day that falls on a non-banking day to the next banking day. The latest shipment date is never extended.
