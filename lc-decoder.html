LC decoder

Paste an MT700 letter of credit or an MT707 amendment. Get a plain-English reading, red and amber watch-outs, and a checklist of the documents you must present.

Runs entirely in your browser. Nothing you paste is uploaded, sent or stored.

Or try a sample (made-up LCs):

Works with the SWIFT format (:31D:) and most bank printouts (31D: Date and Place of Expiry). You can remove names before pasting; the decoder doesn't need them.

New to MT700 fields? Read the MT700 field guide on the UCP 600 page, or work out your deadlines with the LC date calculator.
