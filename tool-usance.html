Usance bill maturity

The due date of a usance draft, adjusted for bank holidays at the place of payment.

Enter bank holidays at the place of payment. Results are only as accurate as the dates you enter.

Under ISBP, "from" and "after" a date both start counting on the following day, so 90 days from 20 October matures on 18 January. If maturity falls on a day the bank is closed, payment is due on the next banking day. This is an estimate. The actual due date can also depend on the wording of the bill, the applicable law and the banking calendar at the place of payment.
