Incoterms® 2020 · ICC rules for the use of trade terms

Delivery terms

Eleven three-letter terms that settle who arranges transport and insurance, who clears customs, and where risk moves from seller to buyer. The term chosen shapes which documents the LC asks for.

Based on ICC Incoterms® 2020 · Last reviewed October 2026

7 terms

Any mode of transport

EXW, FCA, CPT, CIP, DAP, DPU, DDP. Use these for containers and multimodal shipments.

4 terms

Sea and inland waterway only

FAS, FOB, CFR, CIF. Best for bulk cargo loaded directly onto a vessel.

FOB
Free on Board

The seller pays until the goods are loaded on board the vessel at the port of loading. Risk passes once on board. The buyer pays sea freight, insurance and import costs.

Risk passes to buyer: after loaded on board.

A simplified view for learning. Unloading at destination and some handling costs depend on the contract of carriage; always check the ICC Incoterms® 2020 rules for the full allocation.

Terms and the LC documents that usually go with them

TermRisk passes to buyerSeller pays main freightSeller insuresTypical LC transport / insurance documents
EXW
Ex Works
When goods are made available at seller's premisesNoNoUsually none from the seller; often a warehouse or cargo receipt. Rarely suitable for LCs.
FCA
Free Carrier
When handed to buyer's carrier at named placeNoNoForwarder's or carrier's receipt, air waybill, or multimodal document. On-board bill of lading if the parties agree.
CPT
Carriage Paid To
When handed to the first carrierYesNoTransport document from the first carrier, freight prepaid.
CIP
Carriage and Insurance Paid To
When handed to the first carrierYesYes (all-risks cover)Transport document, freight prepaid, plus insurance document (all-risks cover, 110%).
DAP
Delivered at Place
At destination, ready for unloadingYesNoDelivery note or transport document; seller bears transport to destination.
DPU
Delivered at Place Unloaded
At destination, once unloadedYesNoDelivery note confirming goods unloaded at destination.
DDP
Delivered Duty Paid
At destination, ready for unloadingYesNoDelivery note; seller handles import clearance and duties.
FAS
Free Alongside Ship
When placed alongside the vessel at the port of shipmentNoNoDock receipt or mate's receipt showing goods alongside the vessel.
FOB
Free on Board
When loaded on board the vesselNoNoOn-board bill of lading, usually marked freight collect.
CFR
Cost and Freight
When loaded on board the vesselYesNoOn-board bill of lading marked freight prepaid.
CIF
Cost, Insurance and Freight
When loaded on board the vesselYesYes (minimum cover)On-board bill of lading, freight prepaid, plus insurance document (minimum cover, 110%).

Under FCA, the parties can agree that the buyer's carrier issues an on-board bill of lading to the seller, which helps when an LC requires one.
